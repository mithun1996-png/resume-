About

Hello! I am Mithun Patra, a researcher working in the field of Exoplanet atmospheres. My current focus is on reflected light spectroscopy , where I develop forward models to simulate planetary atmospheres and perform retrievals to infer atmospheric properties from observational data My work involves Radiative transfer modeling, cloud and aerosol physics, and spectral analysis. I am experienced in building and optimizing computational models, using tools such as python, high-performance numerical methods, and retrieval framework like POSEIDON and PICASO. I am passionate about advancing our understanding of planetary atmospheres through a combination of theory, modeling, and data analysis.
