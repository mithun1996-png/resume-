About Me

I am a doctoral researcher in exoplanet atmospheric science working on reflected-light spectroscopy, atmospheric retrievals, and cloud microphysics. My research focuses on interpreting observations from current and future missions such as JWST and the Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO).
